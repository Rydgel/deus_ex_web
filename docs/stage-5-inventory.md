## 1. The Training course: three maps, one mission script

`Mission00` (`DeusEx.Mission00`, the DeusExLevelInfo's script for all three maps) runs the course's state machine on a one-second timer. `FirstFrame` zeroes the
player's skill points (`SkillPointsTotal`, `SkillPointsAvail`), knocks out `UNATCOTroop2` (`Tag Test_Subject`) in 00_Training and gives the LAM `Bot1LAM` to the
player in 00_TrainingCombat. `Timer` removes the player's whole inventory once `MunitionsTrooperReady` is set (the FlagTriggers at each map exit), starts
`DL_Lockpick` when one of the two `Cratepick` crates is gone, hides the holo-Page once `M00MeetPage` has played, starts `DL_ReleaseBot2` and
`DL_DemolitionLast` when `SecurityBot2` `LAMBot1`/`LAMBot2` are destroyed and `DL_DefuseFinish` when every `DefuseLAM` is gone, sets the rifle skill to level 3
once `DL_RifleRangeShot1` has played, and shows or hides holo-Jaime from the flags `JaimeAppears` and `JaimeBark_Played`. The map-to-map links are
Teleporters (`00_TrainingCombat#combat`, `00_training#backtotrainer`, `00_trainingfinal#stealth`, `00_trainingCombat#backtocombat`) and the last map's
`MapExit` to `dx`: travel is Stage 6. 70 conversations of the mission list are DataLinks; 18 triggers are placed in 00_Training
([the catalog](script-training-datalinks.md)), 21 in 00_TrainingCombat and 18 in 00_TrainingFinal.

## 2. Liberty Island (01_NYC_UNATCOIsland: 3,658 actors; PlayerStart0 at -4761,10431,-281, yaw -7040)

`Mission01` (script of the map): `Timer` counts the island's 28 terrorists (the `Terrorist`s alive plus the `TerroristCarcass`es not left unconscious by JCDenton): fewer than 23
sets `M01PlayerAggressive` (kept to mission 6); `TerroristCommander_Dead` starts `DL_LeaderKilled` (or `DL_LeaderKilledInSpite` after `DL_LeaderNotKilled`);
`MeetTerrorist_Played` without `PlayerAttackedStatueTerrorist` starts `DL_LeaderNotKilled`; `StatueMissionComplete` runs the mission end: opens `UN_maindoor`, unlocks
`StatueRuinDoors` and `temp_celldoor`, unhides `custodytroop` and `postmissiontroops`, destroys `ThugMale2`, `SecurityBot3` and `GuntherHermann`, kills the remaining
`Terrorist`s and empties their inventories, moves Paul to `PaulTeleport`, destroys `NSFTurret01/02` and the `statue_lasertrap` lasers and sets `bNoAlarm` on
`NSFCam01..03`. `FirstFrame` deletes Paul, `custodytroop` and the commander when `M02Briefing_Played` (a return visit). The objective chain is
carried by conversation events (`AddGoal`, `SetFlag`, `AddSkillPoints`, `Trigger`, `TransferObject`/`CheckObject`, `Choice`, `MoveCamera`).

The player's objective: reach the NSF leader (`TerroristCommander0`, 2931,27,2547) at the top of the Statue, via Paul (the map at the dock) and the undercover agent
Harley Filben on the north dock. The original gives these ways, and the DataLinks say so.
