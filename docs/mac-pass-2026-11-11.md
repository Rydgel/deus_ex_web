**On Metal** the unchanged spec passed (`suite/instructor-metal-1.log`, 23.6 s), as it did 3 of 3 on 2026-11-10.

**Cause: `race`.** On SwiftShader the conversation's start stalls the page for 1.06 to 1.13 s. The worker's simulation runs on
through that stall. `use()` (Playwright's right-click) returns only after it, 0.63 to 0.95 s after it was sent. By then the
greeting has ended (run 3) or is in its last 0.1 s (run 1). The spec's reads can only start after `use()` returns, so sampling them
every 100 ms cannot reach the greeting. This corrects the third pass's reading ("polls backed off to a second apart straddled
it"). The 100 ms polls did not straddle anything; the spec started reading after the greeting. On this headless renderer
it failed all 8 runs of this pass: the whole run, alone, three timeline probes and three logged runs, one of which failed a line
earlier. It also failed all 6 on 2026-11-10, and it passes on Metal. So `harness` would fit as well: the headless shell's
software renderer is not a browser player's.

### Chromium: `lighting`

The error, in the whole run and alone: `expect.poll(() => [canvas.width, canvas.height]).toEqual([926, 603])` received
`[926, 610]` for 5 s, at line 24. The spec selects Inspect, reads the canvas box, sets the viewport so the canvas will be 603
high, and polls the canvas buffer. It passed on 2026-11-10 (`57f28f9f`).

Three probes (`.work/suite-probe/r4/lighting-probe{,2,3}.spec.mjs`, `suite/lighting-probe*`) show where it goes wrong:
* **The measurement is right.** After the switch to Inspect the box is 610 high at once. The viewport the spec computes, 712,
  gives a 603 canvas in every probe that waited before resizing.
* **The page stalls.** In the spec's order, two page reads after the switch took 2.9 s and 2.7 s to answer.
* **The resize arrives late.** Resizing at once, as the spec does: the first read after `setViewportSize(926, 712)` was asked
  108 ms after it and answered **5,911 ms** later, with 603. Every read after that waited about 0.6 s for the page, which
  suggests (inferred, frame times not read) that Inspect at 926 × 603 on SwiftShader takes about that long a frame.

**On Metal** the unchanged spec passed (`suite/lighting-metal-1.log`, 7.3 s).

**Cause: `race`.** On SwiftShader the page is busy for 5.9 s after the switch to Inspect and the resize, so the spec reads the
canvas before the loaded host has applied the new size. The resize lands 0.9 s after the spec's 5 s poll gives up. Why this
spec now fails here, after passing on 2026-11-10, was not traced: whether the stall grew between `57f28f9f` and `881d4cbc`
was not measured.
